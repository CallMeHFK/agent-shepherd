# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as contributors and maintainers commit to making participation in agent-shepherd
a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to a welcoming, respectful,
collaborative environment focused on the project.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being considerate and patient with other people
- Respecting differing viewpoints, ideas, and personal experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or attention of any kind
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Harassment, online or offline
- Publishing others' private information (e.g. a home or work address) without
  explicit permission
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting

## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will
take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they
deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful, including:

- Asking the offending person to stop or remove their behavior
- Removing or editing comments, commits, or other content that is not in line with
  this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently banning contributors who repeatedly misbehave, whether
  or not it was intentional

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies both in project spaces (repo, issues, PRs, calls) and
in personal spaces when someone is representing the project (e.g. using a project
email address, posting on an official account, or acting as a designated
representative at an event).

## Enforcement

Behavior that violates this Code of Conduct can be reported to the project
maintainer via GitHub. All reports will be handled confidentially. Maintainers who
do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith can be held accountable
by the remaining maintainers.

If you are unsure whether something is a violation, ask a maintainer — it is always
better to check than to assume.
